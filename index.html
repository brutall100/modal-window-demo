How it works

  1. 01

    The browser does the hard part

    dialog.showModal() puts the window on top, keeps the focus inside it and makes Esc close it.

  2. 02

    A click outside closes it

    If the click lands on the dialog itself and not on its content, it was on the dark backdrop, so we close.

  3. 03

    CSS makes it pop

    The window grows from 92% to 100% and fades in. Only transform and opacity move, so it stays smooth.
